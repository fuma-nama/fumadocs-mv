## Big idea: one machine, one ride

The section is **one connected system**: a ring-shaped bus (an orbit) around the core. The
four integrations are **stations standing on the ring** at clock positions **09 OpenAPI →
12 Obsidian → 03 Sanity → 06 Story**. The CLI's caret becomes the orange **packet** and rides
the ring clockwise. The camera rides with it: the ring is the **horizon** at the bottom of the
frame, its centre (the core) far below, like walking around a small planet. Going from one
station to the next is a **90° turn of the whole world**: a rotational whip that lands about 91 %
of the way at each block downbeat. At each station the packet climbs off the bus into the input,
triggers the transform, and drops back onto the bus. On bar 56 the camera pulls out to the whole
ring, a clock face with four stations. The four outputs land in the core on the fill's 16ths,
and in the stop the ring tightens into the moon's rim.

Why: "connected" is shown, not stated (one continuous camera move around one structure, paid
off by the reveal). **Follow the dot**: the packet is the film's orange guide (caret → packet
→ core). The ring records the journey like the TOC indicator: a grey rail, and a travelled path
in each block's key colour. Motif rhymes: the clock ring with numerals 01–12 (intro), the TOC
rail (toc), the moon, boxed CONNECT (TACTIC), P1PE leader labels, % tags, chop flap display.

Alternatives rejected: a linear rail (four slides, nothing to pay off at the end); hard cuts
matched on the dot (reads as unrelated slides); upright stations with diagonal pans (loses the
always-forward ride). The rolling ride keeps one screen direction (right) for 12 s.
