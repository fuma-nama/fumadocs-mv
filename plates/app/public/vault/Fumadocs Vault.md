Welcome to the **Fumadocs Vault**, an Obsidian vault rendered by Fumadocs. Every note is a page, and [[Welcome|wikilinks]] become real links.

> [!note] Write in Obsidian
> Keep your notes where you write them. Fumadocs reads the vault, links included: [[hello world]], [[create a link]] and [[Folder/Nested Page|nested pages]].

## Callouts

Obsidian callouts turn into Fumadocs callouts.

> [!tip] Tip
> Use `[[Page#Heading]]` to link to a heading, like [[Welcome#Introduction!!]].

> [!warning] Heads up
> Embeds like `![[Xmas.png]]` are resolved from the vault.

> [!success] Published
> The vault is now a docs site.
